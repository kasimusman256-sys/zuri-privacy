Zuri Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026. Zuri is operated by Kasim Usman, Abuja, Nigeria. Contact: Kasimamaduusman@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details from Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Content you create in Zuri: tasks, notes, profile text and settings.
  • Advertising data: Zuri shows ads through Google AdMob. With your consent where required, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads.

How we use it

To sign you in, save your content and keep it available when you sign in again, and to show ads that support the app. We do not sell your personal data.

Where it is stored

Your account and content are stored with our backend provider, Supabase, and also on your device. Your content is private to your account and is protected so that other users cannot read it. Sign-in is handled by Google. Ads are provided by Google AdMob; see Google's advertising policy.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time.
  • In Zuri, Settings, "Delete all data" clears your tasks, notes and profile details.
  • Where ad consent is required, you can change your choice under "Ad privacy settings" in Settings.
  • To delete your account and all data we hold about you, email Kasimamaduusman@gmail.com from the address linked to your Google account. We will delete it within 30 days.

Retention

We keep your data until you delete it or ask us to delete your account.

Children

Zuri is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
